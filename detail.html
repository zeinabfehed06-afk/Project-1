The Return Of the Bow

Bows made their comeback in style, and people are finding new ways to add them to their daily outfits/looks.

The Bow's Comeback

Bows made their comeback to the fashion industry again. They became a beautiful part of the daily fashion, from statement accessories, simple hair ribbons, they add a soft and romantic touch to different Outfits.

A timeless fashion detail

What makes bows special is how easily they can be styled into different vibes. A simple ribbon can add a touch of elegance to a hairstyle, while bows on clothing can add a girly vibe, or it can also add a personality to the outfit.

Bows are also showing up everywhere nowadays , from handbags to shoes and even jewelry. Sometimes, adding a small bow is all it takes to make a simple outfit look cuter and shows effort.

The best thing about bows is that there are so many ways to wear them. Whether you're going for a cute, girly look or something more classy, a bow can easily bring the whole outfit together.

Bows: A Little Background

Bows have been part of fashion for hundreds of years. Especially back in the 1600s, women used to decorate their clothes, shoes and even their hair with ribbons. Over time, bows became a very popular fashion detail, and now they're making their way back into modern outfits in different styles and a variety of colours.
